These documents define how the optional Human Context module (Human Reality,
D-024 to D-028) would be run safely. They are written now so that the policies
can be reviewed *before* any collection exists. They are procedures and schemas
only (Class A). No practitioner data, no names of professionals, and no emergency
numbers appear here.

## What the module is, and is not

- **Is:** an optional, separately consented, practitioner-led conversation about
  how the practitioner experiences running their business and what makes action
  easier or harder, used only to adapt pacing, sequence and design.
- **Is not:** diagnosis, therapy, counselling, assessment of a person, or a way to
  infer trauma or psychological conditions (D-025, D-026). It never changes a
  severity, a route or a price.
- **Is optional:** every practitioner receives the core service whether or not they
  take part (D-026).

## Principles carried from V1.2

1. The practitioner is the authority on their own experience.
2. Observable business signals never establish a human factor on their own.
3. The practitioner's words are recorded as theirs, not rewritten into clinical
   language.
4. No inference of trauma histories, conditions, diagnoses or motives, by people
   or by AI.
5. The practitioner can skip, edit, withdraw or delete at any time without
   affecting the core service.
6. Detail only as far as the practitioner chooses. Trauma histories are not
   requested; if volunteered, only the practical accommodations the practitioner
   approves are recorded.
