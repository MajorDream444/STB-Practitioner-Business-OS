Behind your dashboard: HAMAL
Proposed future platform

What exists today: the diagnostic method, to be delivered by the HAMAL team by hand with a few authored assistant skills; any implementation would also be done by hand. The approval and verification policies will apply to that work, but nothing enforces them in software, and no paid engagements have been delivered yet. The agent engine, the dashboard and the practitioner-side runtime are not built. HAMAL's own internal team tools are not part of the practitioner's system.

For training schools

SPARK LAB could become the bridge between certification and running a sustainable independent practice. The school offers the support; HAMAL provides the delivery behind the scenes. This is a proposed model: no school agreements exist and no results have been measured.

Potential partnership model · no agreements exist
  1. The gap after certificationGraduates can teach, but few were shown how enquiries, bookings and follow-up work.
  2. An optional next stepEach interested graduate gets a human-led business diagnostic.
  3. The graduate decidesSelf-serve, implementation, referral or no action. The school never chooses.
  4. Owned, not rentedAnything built lives in the graduate's own accounts, tested with their own enquiry.
  5. What the school seesParticipation only: enrolled, diagnostic done, outcome chosen. Never client records.

What exists today, and what we're building toward

Every element on this page carries one of these labels.

Exists today

Real and usable now.

Approved policy

A founder-approved operating rule. Approved is not built: automated enforcement may not exist.

Proposed

Designed concept, not decided or built.

Inactive

Drafted, switched off, collects nothing.

Not built

Future capability shown for explanation.

  • Exists today: the human-led diagnostic method, a candidate 18-check list still being finalised, three source intake schemas not yet merged into one, and a few proven building blocks (single enquiry routing, regression tests that block fake confirmations).
  • Approved policy, enforced by people: four equal outcomes, practitioner approval before anything goes live, end-to-end verification, one-way private-to-shared learning.
  • Not yet: no paid diagnostics delivered, no measured results, no live dashboard, no automated agent engine, no automated approval or verification, no practitioner-side runtime, no school agreements.

The practitioner sees simplicity. HAMAL handles the complexity. The practitioner retains control.
